Impact

Reduced cost in labor and field logistics

Stronger brand trust and perceived client value

Fleet Telematics · 90Crafters
Fleetgame
E2E Developer & Co-founder · Data-to-Action
The problem
Trackers and sensors produced data — but turning that raw signal into operational decisions was the bottleneck. Fleet management was reactive, not intelligence-driven.
What we built
A control tower integrating all trackers: real-time fleet position, fuel consumption, telemetry KPIs, driver performance scores, and custom management reports — connecting raw data to daily governance.
Impact
Measurable reduction in fuel consumption and emissions
Driver recognition program built on performance data
Better trip planning and operational governance
Scaled operations with less effort, more consistency
TelematicsIoT IntegrationDashboardsData-to-ActionFull-Stack
Transport & Tourism · 90Crafters
Sampaio Turismo
E2E Developer · End-to-End Digitalization
The problem
Sales and operations managed via WhatsApp and spreadsheets — seat management, passenger lists, and billing all done manually. High rework and no scalable structure.
What we built
Two integrated fronts: a passenger app for ticket purchase, seat selection, and real-time notifications — plus a web management panel for trips, buses, passenger lists, and billing.
