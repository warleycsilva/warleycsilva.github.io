05 — Case Studies

Operational impact

Corporate Ergonomics · 90Crafters
Superativa
E2E Developer · Operational Automation
The problem
An ergonomics operation fully dependent on manual processes for training sessions, reports, visits, and client management. Growth was being held back by the weight of repetitive administrative work.
What we built
End-to-end system with automated report generation, dedicated access portals for clients and instructors, and centralized custom dashboards — replacing fragmented workflows with a single operational flow.
Impact
Higher service capacity with the same team size
Reduced cost in labor and field logistics
More predictable, standardized operation
Stronger brand trust and perceived client value
Process Automation Report Generation Client Portal Full-Stack
Fleet Telematics · 90Crafters
Fleetgame
E2E Developer & Co-founder · Data-to-Action Platform
The problem
Trackers and sensors produced data — but turning that raw signal into operational decisions was the bottleneck. Fleet management was reactive, not intelligence-driven.
What we built
A control tower integrating all trackers: real-time fleet position, fuel consumption, telemetry KPIs, driver performance scores, and custom management reports — connecting raw data to daily governance.
Impact
Measurable reduction in fuel consumption and emissions
Driver recognition program built on performance data
Better trip planning and operational governance
Scaled operations with less effort, more consistency
Telematics IoT Integration Dashboards Data-to-Action Full-Stack
Transport & Tourism · 90Crafters
Sampaio Turismo
E2E Developer · End-to-End Digitalization
The problem
Sales and operations managed via WhatsApp and spreadsheets — seat management, passenger lists, and billing all done manually. High rework and no scalable structure.
What we built
Two integrated fronts: a passenger app for ticket purchase, seat selection, and real-time notifications — plus a web management panel for trips, buses, passenger lists, and billing.
Impact
Fully digital purchase journey for passengers
Significant reduction in manual customer service effort
Full control over seats, passengers, and revenue
Operation structurally ready to scale
Mobile App React Native Web Backoffice E2E Digitalization
